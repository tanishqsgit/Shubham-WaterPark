# Review visitor-facing content before launch

Audience: the person maintaining the website's copy and images.

1. Read the [application guide](../shubham-waterpark-3d/README.md) and locate
   `src/data/attractions.ts` inside the application folder.
2. Confirm ride names, restrictions, opening times, prices, and contact information
   with the site owner. Do not infer safety instructions from a photograph.
3. Replace placeholders only with approved details. Confirm booking and map links
   reach the intended destination before enabling them.
4. Check that each meaningful photograph has descriptive alt text and a clear caption.
5. Preview at mobile and desktop widths. Check that text remains readable over photos.
6. Use the keyboard to open and close the photo viewer. Check focus remains visible.
7. Enable reduced motion and verify the page remains usable without scroll effects.
8. Record the reviewed revision, actual results, unresolved issues, and reviewer.

This is a review checklist, not a completed accessibility audit or business approval.
The documentation was drafted with AI assistance; human content review is pending.
